01 — Why a metre

Light above.
Conversation below.

The base is the standard 2.2 cm, so the candle sits in the holders you already own.

Care & safety
  1. Use a heavy, heat-resistant holder: a base at least 10 cm wide and a socket at least 4 cm deep.
  2. Keep out of draughts and at least 30 cm from anything above or beside it.
  3. Trim the wick to about 6 mm before each lighting.
  4. Never leave a burning candle unattended. Put it out when 5 cm remain.
  5. Many venues allow only enclosed flames. Check your venue's rules before you order.
Storage

Keep candles flat in their box, somewhere cool (below 25 °C) and out of the sun. A metre of wax will soften and bow if left upright in a warm room.

Returns

Unopened boxes can be returned within 30 days. For weddings, order at least four weeks ahead so there is time to replace anything that arrives damaged.

07 — Questions

Asked often.

Will they fit my candle holders?

Yes, if the holder takes a standard dinner taper: the base is 2.2 cm (7/8 in). Because the candle is a metre tall, choose a holder that is heavy, at least 10 cm across at the base, with a socket at least 4 cm deep.

Will a candle this tall stay straight?

The wax blend is chosen for stiffness. Store the candles flat and cool until the day. Once lit they stay upright; a warm room above 28 °C or direct sun can make any tall candle lean.

Do they drip?

Very little, in still air. Draughts are what make any taper drip, so keep them away from open doors and air vents.

How long do they burn?

About 30 hours from a full metre. For a dinner of five hours you will use roughly the top 15 cm, and can relight them another night.

Will my venue allow them?

Ask before you order. Many venues, especially in the UK and US, allow only flames enclosed in glass. Where open flame is allowed, venues usually want candles on heavy, non-combustible bases and away from exits and drapes. We can send a one-page safety sheet for your venue.

Can I order other colours or heights?

Silk ivory is the only colour for now. For orders of fifty dozen or more we can discuss a custom colour.

When should I order for a wedding?

Four to six weeks before the date. That leaves time for delivery, a test burn, and a replacement if a box arrives damaged.
